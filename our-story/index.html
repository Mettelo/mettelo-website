The question behind Mettelo

How do we connect
knowing with doing?

Learning something and having the opportunity to put it into practice are different experiences.

A workshop can introduce a skill. A project creates space to use it. Working with others brings feedback, responsibility and a chance to explain the decisions behind the work. Together, those experiences can help someone demonstrate more than knowledge alone.

Mettelo brings these parts into one developing ecosystem for people working in, or moving into, Data, AI and Technology. The aim is to make it easier to move between learning, participation and professional growth.

O. Johnson Taiwo founded Mettelo to bridge the gap between technical learning, practical experience, professional visibility and meaningful career opportunities.

A community before a platform

The foundation
is participation.

The wider platform is still developing. The community is where its working model is taking shape.

Workshops, project resources, recorded learning and collaboration spaces already give members ways to get involved. People can share expertise, explore practical work, learn with peers and express interest in contributing or leading.

That participation helps Mettelo explore how learning, contributions, feedback and professional evidence can connect. The ambition is to build around the way people actually develop, rather than asking them to fit into a single pathway.
