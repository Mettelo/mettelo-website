Getting started

Who can benefit from Mettelo?

People starting out, changing careers or already working in Data, AI and Technology. Begin with learning resources, professional connections or a project brief, depending on your goals.

Can I join if I do not feel ready for a project?

Yes. Start with recorded sessions and existing community resources. Read project requirements when you are ready; not every brief is suitable for beginners.

I am already in the community. Do I need to apply again?

You do not need a new community application simply to establish website access. Use one website account, verify your email and complete your profile. Existing private-space access and historical registration matching are handled separately.

Projects & learning

How do project interests and selection work?

Read the brief and source-data rules, sign in with a verified account and complete at least 80% of your saved profile. Then tell us your preferred role, availability and contribution. Independent teams can work on the same brief; each starts when its minimum membership and required roles are met, subject to capacity. Interest does not reserve a place; the team confirms assignments separately.

Where can I find recordings and career resources?

Watch the recorded workshops above or explore Mettelo on YouTube. Application and interview resources are accessed through existing member/community routes; some private spaces have their own access requirements.

Can I contribute as a presenter or experienced professional?

Use Propose a workshop to share a practical session idea, or contact the team about a contribution or collaboration. Explain your topic, intended audience and what participants would learn. Availability and suitability are reviewed separately.

Access & support

What is available now, and what is still developing?

Public project briefs, source guidance, recordings and existing community channels are available. Website accounts, project-interest tracking and saved opportunities are in the V2 preview; public account access is being prepared. Integrated workspaces, Proof and the wider ecosystem are developing in stages.

Does participation guarantee a job, payment or verified Proof?

No. Participation does not guarantee employment, payment, certification or verified Mettelo Proof. Any paid arrangement, project selection or evidence verification process must be communicated separately.

How can I get help or contact the team?

Use the Support page for account, project, community or collaboration enquiries, or email hello@mettelo.com. Include the relevant issue, but do not send passwords, tokens or confidential employer data.
