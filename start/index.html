What is Mettelo?

Whether you’re starting out, changing careers or growing in your field, Mettelo offers a place to connect with peers, access application and interview resources, explore workshops and contribute to practical projects. Community expectations

Professional by nature.
Collaborative by choice.

A shared standard helps everyone learn and contribute with confidence. Start with these expectations and read the full Community Guidelines and Membership Guide.

  • Follow the Community Guidelines and the rules of each space.
  • Treat Mettelo as a collaborative professional community.

A few useful answers

Find your feet.
Ask your questions.

Do I need to be a member to use the resources?

You can explore the linked public resources and recorded workshops. Private community spaces may review applications before granting access.

Is joining the community the same as joining a project?

No. Create and verify your account to use member features. Browse a project brief, complete your profile to at least 80% and apply for Solo or Team participation. Shortlisting is not an offer; Lab access follows acceptance and readiness checks.

Can I start by learning rather than joining a project?

Yes. Start with recorded workshops, career resources or professional conversations. You can explore projects whenever you are ready.

Do I need GitHub experience?

You can begin with workshops and resources without contributing code. For repository contributions, read the project’s documentation and contribution instructions, and ask questions in the community.

How can I volunteer or help lead?

Use the workshop volunteering form to share expertise. For a project, describe your preferred responsibility in your application. Coordinators and permissions are assigned through the official placement process, not by posting an idea.

Can I book a mentor or a CV review here?

This page connects you to resources and the community. Ask about the availability of peer feedback, mentoring or practice sessions there; this website does not provide a booking system.

Why can’t I access a private space?

Lab access requires an admitted place and completed readiness checks. Market invitations cannot bypass capacity, permissions or the three-Lab allowance. External services such as Discord and OneDrive have their own access requirements.

Where is my checklist progress stored?

Only in this browser on this device, when local storage is available. It is not submitted to Mettelo and does not affect membership or project applications. Clearing browser data removes it.

Does membership guarantee employment or a project place?

No. Mettelo supports professional growth and connection. Membership does not guarantee employment or project placement.
