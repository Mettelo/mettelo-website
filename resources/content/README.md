## Choose what you need help with

You do not need to understand the GitHub folder names. Choose the **visa or passport service** that applies to you, then open its step-by-step guide. These are different application processes: **UK visas are decided by the UK Home Office**, while **Nigerian passport services are handled by Nigerian authorities**.

### A. UK Global Talent Visa — for recognised professionals and researchers

The Global Talent route has **different evidence requirements depending on your field**. Select your actual professional category before gathering documents.

| I work in… | Open the complete guide | What the guide helps me do | Supporting resource |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Technology, Data, AI or Software** | **[UK Global Talent Visa — Digital Technology](global-talent/technology.md)** | Understand Exceptional Talent/Promise, identify relevant professional recognition, plan credible contributions and follow the optional 24-month development strategy | [Digital Technology evidence checklist](global-talent/technology-checklist.md) |
| **Arts, Music, Writing, Fashion, Film or Design** | **[UK Global Talent Visa — Arts & Culture](global-talent/arts-and-culture.md)** | Identify the correct artistic assessor, understand professional recognition, find legitimate opportunities and build evidence through the optional 24-month plan | [Arts & Culture evidence checklist](global-talent/arts-and-culture-checklist.md) |
| **Academic Research, Science or Engineering** | **[UK Global Talent Visa — Academia & Research](global-talent/academia-and-research.md)** | Compare eligible academic appointments, approved fellowships, UKRI-funded research and peer review; identify genuine research-development opportunities | [Academia & Research route checklist](global-talent/research-checklist.md) |

**Unsure which Global Talent pathway fits?** Open the **[Global Talent Visa Pathway Selector](global-talent/README.md)** first. Once you choose a pathway, use the **[Global Talent evidence tracker](global-talent/tracker-guide.md)** to organise real achievements, recommendations and outstanding requirements privately.

### C. Nigerian Passport Services in the UK — renewals, appointments and contactless applications

**[Open the Nigerian Passport Renewal & UK Consular Services Guide →](nigerian-passport/passport-renewal-uk.md)**

For Nigerian passport holders in the UK, this guide explains how to identify the correct NIS route, check contactless eligibility, prepare for appointment-based biometrics when required, verify payments and postage instructions, and track an application.

| Your situation | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| **I want to renew my Nigerian passport** | [Passport renewal step-by-step guide](nigerian-passport/passport-renewal-uk.md) |
| **I need to check contactless eligibility** | [Nigeria Immigration Service — Contactless](https://immigration.gov.ng/contactless/) |
| **I need current UK passport appointment or payment instructions** | [Nigeria High Commission London — Passport Requirements](https://www.nigeriahc.org.uk/requirements-for-e-passport/) |
| **My passport is lost, damaged or has incorrect details** | [NIS passport services](https://immigration.gov.ng/passports/) — select the correct application type |

**This is a Nigerian consular service, not a UK visa route.** Contactless and in-person arrangements vary by applicant; use current official instructions before paying, travelling or sending an original passport.

### Before you use these resources

Mettelo provides **free educational information**, not regulated immigration advice and not visa or passport application processing. Requirements, endorsing bodies, costs and application procedures may change. The relevant government and endorsing authority determine eligibility and decisions. **Never publish passports, bank statements, recommendation letters, immigration references or confidential business/customer data in GitHub.**
