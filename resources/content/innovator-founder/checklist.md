## Understand the checklist — what counts as a useful result?

The 20 tasks are a **business and application preparation sequence**, not 20 visa points. Do not mark a business task ready simply because a slide, website or unsigned letter exists.

| Workstream | Concrete activity worth completing | Evidence of actual progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Innovation** | Interview potential UK customers, compare three real alternatives and explain exactly what the proposed product does differently | Interview log, competitor matrix and specific original capability |
| **Founder credibility** | Identify each founder's original contribution, build responsibilities, skills and funding sources | Source-controlled implementation, authentic founder records and team skills |
| **Viability** | Prepare costs based on real supplier prices, delivery time, UK demand and actual accessible resources | Financial model with assumptions and pilot results |
| **Scalability** | Show testable customer acquisition, service operations, employment needs, costs and possible UK/national growth | Milestones, capacity estimates and defensible projections |
| **Endorsing body** | Find body on **current** GOV.UK authorised list and inspect its actual route, terms, cost and document requirements | Dated official eligibility review and submission record if genuinely made |
| **Visa stage** | Confirm English, maintenance, identity, endorsement letter and any conditional family/TB/translation requirements | Actual private documents and verified application instructions |

### Example: distinguish interest from demand

**Weak:** “More than 300 people joined our community, so 300 will pay for the product.”

**Better investigation:** Separate registrations, active users, enquiries, completed pilots and **actual paying customers**. Ask target buyers what they currently spend and what decision process would authorise a purchase. Capture price-testing limitations. A community can be a source of feedback; it does not automatically prove commercial viability.

### Three useful review questions

**Innovation:** If a competitor could reproduce the idea quickly, what technical, process or commercial advantage really remains?  
**Viability:** If first-year sales are half the forecast, what costs and commitments can the founders actually support?  
**Scalability:** What concrete operational changes, hiring, systems and funding would be needed before expansion, and what evidence supports those assumptions?

**Authoritative references:** [Immigration Rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-innovator-founder) · [Endorsing body guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/scale-up-and-innovator-founder-visa-endorsing-bodies-guidance/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-guidance-for-endorsing-bodies-accessible) · [Current authorised body list](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/endorsing-bodies-innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-endorsing-bodies). Recheck every rule before application.

## Practical 20-step preparation checklist

**The steps below link to the actual 13-section Mettelo business guide.** They mix business validation, official route requirements and conditional administrative tasks. They are **not 20 Home Office eligibility points**.

### Phase A — Find a real problem and credible innovation

- [ ] **1. Confirm your immigration circumstances** — age, current permission, potential switching restrictions and relevant deadlines. [Eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa/eligibility) · [Guide §1](guide.md#1-understand-the-innovator-founder-route).
- [ ] **2. Select a specific UK customer problem** — investigate actual repeated workflow failures using [six discovery methods](guide.md#3-how-to-find-real-problems-in-the-uk).
- [ ] **3. Interview independent prospective customers** — record recurring incidents, existing workaround, buyer roles and measured costs where known. [Customer interviews](guide.md#6-customer-discovery-and-validation-that-produces-real-evidence).
- [ ] **4. Compare named competitors and the status quo** — include real products and manual processes; document a genuine unmet need. [Innovation test](guide.md#5-test-whether-the-idea-is-really-innovative).
- [ ] **5. Identify the core innovation** — explain exactly how it differs, why meaningful, who created it and how it can be delivered by founders. [Innovation test](guide.md#5-test-whether-the-idea-is-really-innovative).

### Phase B — Demonstrate viability and scalability

- [ ] **6. Build a testable prototype** — with permission-cleared or synthetic data, documented architecture, quality and safeguards. [90-day plan](guide.md#9-the-first-90-days--practical-implementation-plan).
- [ ] **7. Run genuine buyer pilots** — define a baseline and success/failure measure before testing; document the results honestly. [Evidence ladder](guide.md#6-customer-discovery-and-validation-that-produces-real-evidence).
- [ ] **8. Show genuine founder and team capability** — who is instrumental in the business, who develops the core idea and what they can actually deliver. [Business case](guide.md#7-build-a-defensible-innovative-viable-and-scalable-business-case).
- [ ] **9. Prepare a realistic funding and cash-flow plan** — document actual available funds, quotes, costs and their origins. [Financial model](guide.md#7-build-a-defensible-innovative-viable-and-scalable-business-case).
- [ ] **10. Develop a repeatable scale plan** — customer acquisition, pricing, operational capacity, UK growth and plausible recruitment triggers. [Scalability](guide.md#7-build-a-defensible-innovative-viable-and-scalable-business-case).
- [ ] **11. Check IP, licences, data protection and regulation** — document rights, contracts and restrictions relevant to your sector. [Business resources](guide.md#8-uk-problem-research-funding-and-support-directory).
- [ ] **12. Draft a credible business plan** — innovation, viability, scalability, market evidence, competitors, risks, founder role and financial scenarios. Follow any **current endorser-specific forecast period**, rather than assuming three-year projections are universally mandatory. [Business plan outline](guide.md#7-build-a-defensible-innovative-viable-and-scalable-business-case).

### Phase C — Check endorsement and visa eligibility

- [ ] **13. Confirm the current authorised endorsing organisation** — examine the [live government list](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/endorsing-bodies-innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-endorsing-bodies), then read the provider's own eligibility and prices. [Endorsers](guide.md#11-approach-authorised-endorsing-bodies-carefully).
- [ ] **14. Prepare genuine provider-specific material** — submit only what a real assessment requests and preserve your own original work and permission-cleared supporting records.
- [ ] **15. Obtain and check an actual endorsement** — where approved, verify all required details, date, signatory and the no-more-than-three-month immigration timing rule. [Immigration Rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-innovator-founder).
- [ ] **16. Confirm English and identity requirements** — prepare current passport and required B2 proof or valid exception. [Official eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa/eligibility).
- [ ] **17. Check the personal maintenance funds rule** — generally £1,270 for 28 consecutive days where applicable; distinguish personal savings from venture funds. [Official eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa/eligibility).
- [ ] **18. Check conditional documents** — TB test, translations, dependent applications or relevant sponsor/scholarship consent **only where current rules require them**. [Official document list](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa/documents-youll-need-to-apply).
- [ ] **19. Budget and complete the actual visa application** — fees, Immigration Health Surcharge, identity process and required submission, following the actual form. [GOV.UK overview](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa).
- [ ] **20. Preserve real decisions and required contact meetings** — if granted, record endorsed business progress and schedule at least two genuine contact-point meetings during permission. [Immigration Rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-innovator-founder).

**Go/no-go rule:** If you cannot demonstrate meaningful innovation, credible commercial viability or a genuine founder role, record the gap and continue development; do not substitute a purchased letter or invented sales record.

## Status, deadlines and evidence fields

Use five useful tracking fields: **What I need to prepare**, **Status**, **Target date**, **Saved file link** and **Outstanding issue / guide section**. **Ready** means you have personally reviewed a task; it is not an endorsing body or UKVI decision.

| What I need to prepare | Status | Target date | Saved file link | Reminder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Paste one task] | To do | YYYY-MM-DD | [Private evidence location] | [Outstanding details] |

Use **Not needed** only for a condition that demonstrably does not apply. Retain the actual evidence in an access-controlled location.

## Progress log

Your private progress log records **Date**, **What I did and the actual result**, **Evidence file link**, **My next step**, and **Target date**.

| Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | [Actual action and evidence-supported outcome] | [Private link] | [Next action] | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Important

This checklist helps organise real business and immigration preparation; it is **not** a separate legal eligibility test and cannot guarantee endorsement, visa approval or settlement. Verify rules and required evidence against current [GOV.UK Innovator Founder guidance](https://www.gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa) and the relevant [authorised endorsing body](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/endorsing-bodies-innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas/innovator-founder-and-scale-up-visas-endorsing-bodies).
