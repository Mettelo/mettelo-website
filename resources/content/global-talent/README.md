## Choose by your *actual* work and recognition

| Your professional background | Main guide | Key distinction | Checklist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Digital products, technology engineering, AI/software/data infrastructure, commercial technology leadership | [**Digital Technology**](technology.md) | Tech Nation assesses technology recognition and specific qualifying work; needs **three expert recommendations** and separate criteria evidence | [Technology checklist](technology-checklist.md) |
| Music, writing, dance, theatre, visual arts, photography, film/TV, fashion, design, architecture | [**Arts & Culture**](arts-and-culture.md) | **Correct creative discipline matters:** Arts Council England and specialist assessors apply distinct requirements | [Arts checklist](arts-and-culture-checklist.md) |
| Academic/industrial research, science, engineering, humanities, social science and medicine | [**Academia & Research**](academia-and-research.md) | Distinct routes for **eligible appointments, approved fellowships, UKRI funded work and full peer review** | [Research checklist](research-checklist.md) |

### Important: do not transfer evidence rules between paths

A technology engineer's ten-document framework does not become the research peer-review requirement. An artistic prize must meet the **particular discipline's** rules. An approved research grant requires an eligible **funder, UK host and actual research role**, not just an academic CV.

## A simple preparation workflow

| Step | Action | Useful output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Route | Read official eligibility and choose the correct professional category | Correct assessor and talent/promise/fast-track route |
| 2 — Evidence | Compare real achievements with explicit requirements | Evidence gap map |
| 3 — Develop | If gaps exist, follow the [24-month development plan](technology.md#10-your-detailed-24-month-implementation-plan), [arts plan](arts-and-culture.md#16-24-month-practical-development-plan) or [research plan](academia-and-research.md#5-detailed-24-month-development-plan) | Genuine work and recognition, not invented evidence |
| 4 — Track | Use the [private evidence tracker](tracker-guide.md) | Dated source/author/criterion/status and next action |
| 5 — Check | Confirm document limits, authentic recommendations, immigration timing and current rules | Honest go/no-go decision |
| 6 — Apply | Follow the relevant official endorsement/visa entry point | Valid submission only when personally eligible |

**The 24-month tables are optional Mettelo career-building programmes.** They are not government waiting periods, application deadlines or guarantees. Some applicants already meet requirements; others may need longer.
