# Digital Technology — Practical Evidence Checklist

[← Tracker guide](tracker-guide.md) · [← Pathway guide](technology.md) · [← Global Talent resources](README.md)

> **How to use:** Make a private copy, record genuine supporting evidence and check your chosen criteria against current GOV.UK and Tech Nation rules. This checklist helps organise your application; completing it does not establish eligibility.

**Statuses:** To do · Working on it · Ready · Not needed (only when genuinely inapplicable).

**For each item record privately:** target date, a link to your private evidence file, and notes about what is missing. Mark Ready only after checking the actual evidence and current official requirements.

## How to prepare credible technology evidence — practical explanations

Use the tasks below to prepare a credible evidence pack. **Before marking anything Ready**, match each item to the **current Tech Nation/GOV.UK rules**, not merely to an activity you completed. Keep originals in an access-controlled folder, with a separate record of document ownership and what you personally contributed.

| What the task means | Specific action | What an authentic record might contain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Choose Talent/Promise and two optional areas** | Review which field of digital technology you actually work in, your recognition, innovation, product-led contributions, outside-work field impact or research; map to the correct category | Criteria matrix distinguishing actual recognition from desired future activity |
| **Demonstrate mandatory recognition** | Seek independently verifiable evidence of genuine professional recognition; consider whether reviewers, selection panels or trusted industry sources recognised actual work | Real dated professional award, qualified published evaluation or other admissible recognition, not a self-issued badge |
| **Product contributions** | Identify exact original work, confirm employer permissions, obtain authorised product/technical confirmation of your responsibility and genuine impact | Credited release, technical review, permitted user/quality data |
| **Open-source activity** | Contribute significant tested work to an established project and document peer acknowledgement if claiming that route | Issue/PR, meaningful code, maintainer review and actual merge |
| **Independent professional activity** | Use legitimate community/research speaking or mentoring only when it actually aligns with the chosen optional criterion | Actual external selection, dated organisers' records and independently verified outcomes |
| **Expert letters** | Identify appropriate experts who have known your work for required time and can independently describe specific achievements | Genuine letters meeting present authorship, signature and credentials requirements |
| **Document count and format** | Give each proposed evidence item one ID, correct criterion, date/source and maximum page count | Private inventory of up to ten permissible items, without prohibited re-use |

### How to use an evidence quality check

For each source item ask: **What exact criterion does this support? Is the work genuinely mine? Who independent of me confirms it? Is it recent enough? Does the underlying material meet the current page and authorship rules?** A repository, LinkedIn post or community membership count is not proof of recognition by itself.

**Follow the detailed [24-month Digital Technology guide](technology.md)** for practical monthly activities and named organisations; this checklist is the *final evidence check*, not another competing plan.

[Official Digital Technology eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-digital-technology/eligibility) · [Documents](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-digital-technology/documents-you-need-to-apply-endorsement).

## Your 18 preparation tasks

- [ ] **1.** Choose talent or promise and the two additional criteria you will use.
- [ ] **2.** Prepare your CV within three A4 pages.
- [ ] **3.** Get expert letter 1 with credentials, contacts and signature.
- [ ] **4.** Get expert letter 2 with different examples, credentials and signature.
- [ ] **5.** Get expert letter 3 with different examples, credentials, contacts and signature.
- [ ] **6.** Check that all three experts have known your work for at least twelve months.
- [ ] **7.** Gather at least two documents showing recognition in the last five years.
- [ ] **8.** Gather at least two documents for your first additional criterion.
- [ ] **9.** Gather at least two documents for your second additional criterion.
- [ ] **10.** Check the evidence cap: ten documents, three A4 pages each, no reuse across criteria.
- [ ] **11.** If applicable, gather founder or senior executive business connection records.
- [ ] **12.** If needed, obtain the required English translations.
- [ ] **13.** Personally prepare the form answers under Tech Nation’s no AI and no template instructions.
- [ ] **14.** Check letter contents and evidence against the current official instructions.
- [ ] **15.** Complete endorsement form, fee and required document handoff; save confirmation.
- [ ] **16.** Record endorsement decision and date; check the visa application deadline.
- [ ] **17.** Prepare passport, visa answers and any conditional visa documents.
- [ ] **18.** Complete visa payment, assigned identity check and supporting document steps.
## Personal progress log (keep private)

Use these columns in a private spreadsheet or notes file: **Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date**. Each row should record one genuine action or outcome.

## Official sources

Confirm current requirements using the [GOV.UK Global Talent guidance](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent) and the relevant endorsement route. Requirements can change; **live official eligibility rules always take priority**.
