# Academia and Research — Practical Evidence Checklist

[← Tracker guide](tracker-guide.md) · [← Pathway guide](academia-and-research.md) · [← Global Talent resources](README.md)

> **How to use:** Select one actual research endorsement route, check its specific evidence requirements and make a private record. Marking a task ready is not confirmation of eligibility.

**Statuses:** To do · Working on it · Ready · Not needed (only when genuinely inapplicable).

**For each item record privately:** target date, a link to your private evidence file, and notes about what is missing. Mark Ready only after checking the actual evidence and current official requirements.

## Read this before selecting a checklist route

**Research Global Talent is not an ordinary evidence portfolio.** The appointment, individual fellowship, UKRI endorsed-funder and peer-review routes have different eligibility and documentary tests. Only tick the subsection **actually relevant to the route you qualify for**. An awarded fellowship is different from having applied; a grant named on a database is different from being personally employed on an eligible project.

| Your route | What you need to ask | Who can reliably confirm it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appointment** | Is it an accepted eligible research role at an approved UK institution, with essential PhD/equivalent standard and proper recruitment procedure? | UK employing HR and actual job description |
| **Approved fellowship** | Does the **exact awarded scheme title** appear on the list, and do its dates/category fit? | Awarding institution and approved fellowship list |
| **UKRI endorsed funder** | Is the host and funder approved, and does this real grant/role meet value/duration, working-time and remaining agreement tests? | Actual principal investigator, university grants/HR office |
| **Peer review — Promise** | Am I an early-career active researcher with PhD/equivalent expertise and genuine eminent UK-resident expert knowledge of my work? | Real UK research expert and official route guidance |
| **Peer review — Talent** | Is there established research leadership and the required second independent objective senior UK expert assessment? | Qualified independent expert authors |

### What to do if you're missing the key evidence

If you lack a qualifying UK research appointment, apply to **genuine research openings**, not an invented HR letter. If your fellowship is not on the named list, confirm whether another route is independently available. If you're not part of an eligible funded UK project, finding its grant identifier alone will not solve that. For full peer review, work on real original research and build authentic expert relationships rather than soliciting signatures from strangers.

**Detailed route development:** [Research Global Talent guide and optional 24-month plan](academia-and-research.md). The plan is **career development**, not a Home Office qualifying period.

[Research route overview](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-researcher-academic/overview) · [Peer review](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-researcher-academic/peer-review) · [UKRI funder](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent-researcher-academic/uk-research-innovation-endorsement).

## Choose one route first

Use **only the subsection for your chosen endorsement route**; the four routes do not share the same evidence requirements. Exceptional Talent and Exceptional Promise peer review have different recommendation-letter requirements.

### Appointment

- [ ] **1.** Verify the approved institution and actual qualifying appointment.
- [ ] **2.** Obtain the full job description showing the required research experience.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the authorised HR guarantee with all required recruitment confirmations.
- [ ] **4.** Check that you accepted the offer and that position and department match.
- [ ] **5.** Verify all route conditions against the current appointment instructions.
- [ ] **6.** Check the issued documents, signatory and accurate identity details.

### Approved fellowship

- [ ] **1.** Match the exact fellowship to the current approved list.
- [ ] **2.** Confirm the talent or promise category attached to that fellowship.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the official fellowship award letter.
- [ ] **4.** Check that the award dates meet the eligibility window.
- [ ] **5.** Check your name, awarding body and exact programme identity.
- [ ] **6.** Check the readable issued award letter against the current route instructions.

### UKRI funded role

- [ ] **1.** Verify the exact approved funder and UK host.
- [ ] **2.** Check qualifying grant type, at least £30,000 and at least two years.
- [ ] **3.** Check role, experience, at least half working time and one year remaining agreement.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain approved award database link or required funder letter.
- [ ] **5.** Obtain the authorised HR guarantee covering every required confirmation.
- [ ] **6.** Check all grants relied on and the current role and recruitment conditions.

### Peer review promise

- [ ] **1.** Confirm active research, approved discipline and PhD or equivalent experience.
- [ ] **2.** Check exceptional promise and early career standing.
- [ ] **3.** Prepare the typed research CV within three A4 pages.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain the main eligible UK resident eminent expert’s recommendation.
- [ ] **5.** Check letter contents, signature, date, credentials and contacts.
- [ ] **6.** Follow the selected academy’s form and any specific additional document instructions.

### Peer review talent

- [ ] **1.** Confirm active research, approved discipline, experience and exceptional standing.
- [ ] **2.** Prepare the typed research CV within three A4 pages.
- [ ] **3.** Obtain the main eligible UK resident eminent expert’s recommendation.
- [ ] **4.** Obtain the separate eligible senior UK expert’s objective assessment.
- [ ] **5.** Check both letters, independent assessment wording, credentials and contacts.
- [ ] **6.** Follow the selected academy’s form and any specific additional document instructions.

## Common follow-up items

Complete the relevant preparation items for your route, subject to current official requirements.

- [ ] **1.** If needed, obtain required English translations or identity clarifications.
- [ ] **2.** Complete endorsement form, fee and required document handoff; save confirmation.
- [ ] **3.** Record endorsement decision and date; check the visa application deadline.
- [ ] **4.** Prepare passport, visa answers and any conditional visa documents.
- [ ] **5.** Complete visa payment and supporting document steps.
- [ ] **6.** Complete the assigned identity or biometrics process and monitor the decision.

## Personal progress log (keep private)

Use these columns in a private spreadsheet or notes file: **Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date**. Each row should record one genuine action or outcome.

## Official sources

Confirm current requirements using the [GOV.UK Global Talent guidance](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent) and the relevant endorsement route. Requirements can change; **live official eligibility rules always take priority**.
