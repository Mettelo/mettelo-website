## 3. Check whether the evidence belongs to the requirement

Ask: **Which exact current criterion? Why is this document credible? What did I personally do? Is an independent author/organisation responsible? Is it within the official recency and document limits?** If it fails, record the gap instead of manufacturing a letter or increasing a metric.

### Evidence quality and privacy

- Keep referee contact details, employment records, confidential business measures, immigration status and original identity documents private.
- Preserve the actual origin, issue date, permission to share and author of each record.
- For peer review, distinguish the personal UK eminent expert letter from any separate objective exceptional-talent letter.
- For technology, do not reuse the same evidence item for multiple optional criteria.
- For general arts, check professional appearances, reviews and prizes **against the correct discipline's rules**.
- If applying from the UK, seek regulated advice where permission is close to expiring; an endorsement-only application does not automatically extend leave.
