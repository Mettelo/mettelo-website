# Arts and Culture — Practical Evidence Checklist

[← Tracker guide](tracker-guide.md) · [← Pathway guide](arts-and-culture.md) · [← Global Talent resources](README.md)

> **How to use:** Make a private copy and identify the correct creative discipline and assessor before collecting documents. A completed checklist does not prove Global Talent eligibility.

**Statuses:** To do · Working on it · Ready · Not needed (only when genuinely inapplicable).

**For each item record privately:** target date, a link to your private evidence file, and notes about what is missing. Mark Ready only after checking the actual evidence and current official requirements.

## What the checklist items actually mean

The checklist contains 18 preparation tasks. Use this table **before** ticking each item; the right answer depends on **general arts versus a specialist assessor**, the correct talent/promise criteria, and what independent evidence exists.

| Checklist group | Practical way to verify it | Common false assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Correct discipline** | Match actual professional practice to [GOV.UK's arts discipline list](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/global-talent-endorsing-bodies/disciplines-covered-by-arts-council-england-or-its-associated-bodies) | A film/TV or fashion applicant can automatically use the general Arts Council route |
| **Professional record / country coverage** | List each credited professional appearance, exhibition, programme, review and country with organiser | Social media visibility equals an international professional track record |
| **Three suitable recommendations** | Verify two real established arts organisations (one UK-based) and actual artistic working relationships; check third organisation/expert separately | An unfamiliar famous person can sign a convincing generic letter |
| **Independent critical reviews** | Find the actual bylined evaluation of your artistic work, publication details, critic and country | Press release, paid advertisement or artist interview counts as critical appraisal |
| **International prize** | Check formal jury, award name, level and whether a Talent win or Promise nomination/shortlist meets rules | An arts grant or residency bursary automatically qualifies as an international prize |
| **Professional presentations** | Keep official programme, curator/producer, credit and eligible country and significance records | Paying to exhibit or hosting a livestream always qualifies |
| **Evidence limits** | Index every genuine item by date, source, criterion, attribution and original page count | Ten unrelated screenshots provide enough evidence |

## Your 18 preparation tasks

- [ ] **1.** Confirm that general arts is the correct route for your discipline.
- [ ] **2.** Check talent or promise, professional history and required country coverage.
- [ ] **3.** Prepare a typed CV with professional career and education.
- [ ] **4.** Get a letter from an eligible UK arts organisation you worked with artistically.
- [ ] **5.** Get a letter from a second eligible expert arts organisation you worked with.
- [ ] **6.** Get a third eligible organisation or expert letter based on actual artistic work together.
- [ ] **7.** Check all three letters: content, signature, date, contacts and author credentials.
- [ ] **8.** Choose at least two evidence categories: critical media, prizes or professional appearances.
- [ ] **9.** If using media, collect at least two qualifying critical reviews and attribution.
- [ ] **10.** If using prizes, verify the qualifying award or permitted promise nomination.
- [ ] **11.** If using appearances, gather professional presentation and required source proof.
- [ ] **12.** Check at most ten single evidence pieces, within five years and two A4 pages each.
- [ ] **13.** If needed, obtain group contribution confirmations and required translations.
- [ ] **14.** Check the full current general arts requirements before submitting.
- [ ] **15.** Complete endorsement form, fee and required document handoff; save confirmation.
- [ ] **16.** Record endorsement decision and date; check the visa application deadline.
- [ ] **17.** Prepare passport, visa answers and any conditional visa documents.
- [ ] **18.** Complete visa payment, assigned identity check and supporting document steps.
## Personal progress log (keep private)

Use these columns in a private spreadsheet or notes file: **Date | What I did and the actual result | Evidence file link | My next step | Target date**. Each row should record one genuine action or outcome.

## Official sources

Confirm current requirements using the [GOV.UK Global Talent guidance](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent) and the relevant endorsement route. Requirements can change; **live official eligibility rules always take priority**.
