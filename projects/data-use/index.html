Data sources & reuse rules

A dataset being publicly accessible does not automatically permit copying, redistribution or commercial use.

Before you use project data

  1. Read the master repository’s data workspace and project governance instructions.
  2. Check the original provider, licence, access conditions and any restrictions on your intended use.
  3. Record provenance, download date, data period, limitations and required attribution.
  4. Keep raw data unchanged and make transformations reproducible.
  5. Do not redistribute, publish, upload to external AI tools or commercialise restricted resources without permission.
  6. If permission or licensing is unclear, ask Mettelo before using or sharing the data.

Describe the engagement accurately

Where the master brief describes a simulated engagement, do not present the simulated organisation as a real Mettelo client or the work as paid employment. Distinguish team outputs from your individual contribution.

Project-specific instructions take priority

Each project brief links to published source and governance guidance where verified. Missing licence metadata is not permission to reuse a dataset. A repository’s software licence does not necessarily cover its datasets.
