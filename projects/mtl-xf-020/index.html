Cross-FunctionalPROJECT BRIEF

Housing Market Affordability, Development & Investment Risk Intelligence

A housing strategy team needs to identify areas where sustained market-price pressure warrants closer affordability or development analysis. Build a housing-market dashboard and investigation shortlist, making clear which income, supply or planning evidence is still needed.

Housing / Economic Development / InvestmentMTL-XF-020
The challengeData & reuseCapabilitiesRolesTeams
01 · THE CHALLENGE

The problem to work on

Build a housing-market intelligence product that distinguishes sustained market pressure from one-period price movement and clearly separates price evidence from true affordability.

Use case

Supports housing strategy and development planning by identifying areas that warrant deeper affordability, supply or planning analysis before intervention decisions are considered.
