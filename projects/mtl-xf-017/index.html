Cross-FunctionalPROJECT BRIEF

Flood Defence Asset, Investment & Risk Prioritisation Intelligence

A flood-investment team needs to identify which schemes require earlier engineering, funding or delivery review. Build a programme dashboard that brings investment plans, delivery information and evidence gaps together to support a transparent review queue.

Environment / Flood Risk / Capital InvestmentMTL-XF-017
The challengeData & reuseCapabilitiesRolesTeams
01 · THE CHALLENGE

The problem to work on

Create a transparent flood-investment portfolio view that helps leaders identify schemes requiring earlier review while making funding, delivery and evidence gaps visible.

Use case

Supports capital-programme reviews by showing which schemes need engineering review, funding challenge or additional evidence before investment prioritisation.
