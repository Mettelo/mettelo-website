Business AnalysisPROJECT BRIEF

Employee Onboarding & Access Integration Analysis

HR, service tickets and identity systems do not reliably coordinate joiners, changed dates and leavers. Specify interface behaviour, data mappings, event flows and recovery controls for a bounded integration handover.

Professional Services / Workforce / Identity and AccessMTL-BA-044
The challengeData & reuseCapabilitiesRolesTeams
01 · THE CHALLENGE

The problem to work on

Harrowgate needs authoritative employment events, reliable identifiers and accountable failure handling; ticket closure or directory acceptance alone does not establish worker readiness or complete revocation.

Use case

Engineering, HR and Security use the specification to review integration contracts, estimate work and identify blockers to a controlled introduction.
