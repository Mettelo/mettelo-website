Artificial IntelligencePROJECT BRIEF

Data Quality Investigation Copilot

A data operations team receives failed validation checks but still spends significant time investigating what failed, what may be affected and what evidence to collect next. Build a local AI copilot that combines deterministic data-quality evidence with bounded AI assistance to produce evidence-linked investigation drafts, safe next steps and human-reviewed incident reports.

The challengeData & reuseCapabilitiesRolesTeams
01 · THE CHALLENGE

The problem to work on

Validation identifies failed rules but does not reliably explain likely causes, downstream impact or the evidence an engineer should investigate next.

Use case

A data operations analyst opens a failed batch, inspects rule evidence and declared lineage, generates an evidence-linked investigation draft, edits or rejects hypotheses and exports a reviewed incident report with an accountable owner.

02 · SCOPE & RESOURCES
