Project library / MTL-DS-009
Data SciencePROJECT BRIEF

London Fire Incident Response Demand & Risk Modelling

A fire-service planning team needs to understand which recorded incident characteristics are associated with higher response demand. Build an explainable classification model and assess its reliability so planners can investigate demand patterns responsibly.

Emergency Services / Public SectorMTL-DS-009

01 · THE CHALLENGE

The problem to work on

Develop an explainable classification model that identifies incidents associated with higher operational response demand while controlling leakage and model risk.
