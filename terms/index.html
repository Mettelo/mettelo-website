01 / TERMS OF USE

Scope & current services

These proposed terms cover the Mettelo public website and participation through its linked community resources. They are a draft for review, not an effective agreement. The operator’s legal identity, contact details and applicable legal framework must be confirmed before publication as effective terms.

Mettelo is developing a professional ecosystem for learning, collaboration, practical experience and career development. Current entry points include external membership and project-interest forms, a Project Library, learning resources, recorded workshops and community spaces.

This public website does not provide member accounts, paid subscriptions, a verified professional-evidence system or an employment placement service. Developing ecosystem capabilities are ambitions, not a promise of currently available services.

02 / TERMS OF USE

Membership & access

Community membership and project participation are separate applications. Applying does not automatically grant access to private spaces or a project role. Access may require approval and may depend on available capacity, suitability and the requirements of the relevant activity.

  • Provide accurate application information and keep relevant organisers informed of material changes.
  • Read the Community Guidelines and any activity-specific requirements before participating.
  • Use approved invitations responsibly. Do not share private access links or another person’s personal information without permission.

Eligibility, any minimum age and the process for reviewing access decisions require confirmation before these terms become effective.

03 / TERMS OF USE

Professional conduct

Treat other people respectfully and professionally. Do not harass, discriminate, impersonate others, misrepresent your experience, spam community spaces or share unlawful material. Protect confidential information and use data and tools responsibly.

Follow the published Community Guidelines and the rules of each external community service. Organisers may need to address harmful conduct or restrict access to protect participants. The moderation process, reporting route and review arrangements remain subject to operational approval.

04 / TERMS OF USE

Projects & collaboration

Browse the existing Project Library ↗ and use the separate project participation form to express interest. An expression of interest is not a confirmed role, employment contract or commitment to deliver paid work.

Before starting, agree the project scope, responsibilities, time commitment, data permissions, review process and contribution rules with the project lead. Clarify any compensation or other contractual arrangements separately; do not assume that participation is paid.

Contribute work you can explain and are authorised to share. Acknowledge collaborators and disclose material assistance, including AI-generated work where relevant. Do not upload personal, confidential or proprietary data without appropriate permission.

05 / TERMS OF USE

Contributions & ownership

Contributors retain ownership of their own original work unless a separate, valid agreement provides otherwise. Respect third-party intellectual property, attribution requirements and the licence attached to each repository or resource.

Before contributing, check the applicable project licence or contributor agreement. Agree any rights needed to use, combine, publish or showcase contributions. This draft does not grant Mettelo a blanket licence to publish your work, name, photograph or professional profile.

Do not present team work as solely your own. Obtain any necessary permission before publishing project outputs or using them in a portfolio. Where ownership or publication rights are unclear, resolve them before sharing the material.

06 / TERMS OF USE

External services & resources

Applications use Tally; resource and volunteering links use Microsoft OneDrive; projects and code use GitHub; recordings use YouTube; community access may involve Discord or WhatsApp. The public website links to these services rather than operating them.

Each provider has its own terms, privacy practices and access requirements. Review those before submitting information or joining a space. External content, invitations and availability may change. Linking to a resource does not promise its accuracy, suitability or continued availability.

Use learning material critically and assess opportunities independently. Any employment, sponsorship or commercial arrangement requires its own checks and agreement with the relevant party.

07 / TERMS OF USE

Outcomes, availability & limitations

Membership does not guarantee employment, sponsorship, project placement or professional outcomes. Learning, mentoring, peer feedback, networking and project contributions may support development, but results depend on individual circumstances and available opportunities.

Mettelo is developing. Activities, resources and community access may change or pause. No uninterrupted availability, specific response time, accreditation or certification is promised through this website.

Resources and peer guidance are general information. Assess their suitability for your circumstances and seek qualified advice where needed. Nothing in this draft is intended to limit rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be limited. Any proposed limitations of liability require legal review before these terms become effective.

08 / TERMS OF USE

Privacy & browser storage — pending review

A V1 Privacy Policy is not yet approved. The website currently loads local assets and the Start Here checklist stores progress in your browser using local storage. The reviewed source contains no optional analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting behaviour and third-party services require separate verification.

The checklist does not create an account or send your progress to Mettelo. Use its reset control or clear this website’s browser storage to remove saved progress. External application and community services handle information under their own policies; those policies do not replace Mettelo’s own privacy responsibilities.

Operator identity, privacy contact, purposes, lawful bases where applicable, retention arrangements and international-transfer safeguards must be confirmed before a complete Privacy Policy can be approved.

09 / TERMS OF USE

Questions, reporting & next review

A verified public enquiry and privacy-request contact has not yet been confirmed. This page does not provide a working contact form or a verified privacy email. Publishing a reliable route for general enquiries, moderation reports, project questions and privacy requests is a launch requirement.

Existing community members can use the organiser or moderator route already provided within their approved community space for routine support. Do not post sensitive information publicly. Project participants should use the contact route supplied for their project.

This draft has no effective date. Before publication, confirm the operator, eligibility, governing law, contact and moderation arrangements, project contribution rights and the final legal wording. Material changes should be clearly communicated with a confirmed effective date.
